# GlobalGlance YouTube Workflow

A lightweight, repeatable process for organizing GlobalGlance channel ideas and videos. Keep the workflow simple enough to manage from an iPhone. Use YouTube Studio or other tools you already have access to; no paid service or hosting is required.

## 1. Capture an idea

Write down a short working title and the core question, story, or information the video should deliver. Add a sentence on why it may be useful or interesting to the intended viewer. Capture ideas in this document, a notes app, or another free tool you already use.

**Idea check:** Is the topic clear, appropriate for the channel, and possible to research and explain responsibly?

## 2. Choose one idea

Pick an idea based on interest, audience usefulness, available time, and whether the facts can be checked. Keep a small list of ideas rather than trying to plan the whole channel at once. If an idea is not a fit, archive or remove it without guilt.

## 3. Research and outline

- Use reliable, relevant sources and check important claims against more than one source when practical.
- Keep a simple list of source names and links in your working notes.
- Separate verified facts from opinion, uncertainty, or speculation.
- Draft a short outline: opening, main points, and takeaway.
- Credit source material and respect copyright. Use original or properly licensed visuals and audio.

Do not put passwords, API keys, private account details, or sensitive personal information in research notes or public repository files.

## 4. Prepare the video

Make a brief checklist for the recording or editing session: outline ready, equipment charged, quiet location, and any needed assets available. A phone is enough for a simple start. Keep the format and production expectations realistic for your time and experience.

## 5. Review before publishing

Watch or review the finished video before it goes public. Check that the title and description are accurate, sources and credits are included where appropriate, audio is understandable, and there is no private information visible or audible. Check that the thumbnail and other assets are ones you may use.

## 6. Publish and organize

Use YouTube Studio, including its mobile experience where available, to upload and manage the video. Review the current YouTube interface and options as they may change. Add an accurate title, description, thumbnail, and audience settings. Choose visibility intentionally (private, unlisted, or public) and confirm before publishing.

After publishing, record the video title, link, publication date, and any follow-up action in a private planning note or a public project tracker only if it contains no sensitive information. Do not commit credentials or private channel information to this repository.

## 7. Learn and improve

After a reasonable interval, check available free analytics if useful. Focus on one lesson—such as which topics viewers respond to or whether the workflow was manageable—rather than chasing every metric. Use that lesson to improve the next idea or update this workflow.

## Simple iPhone checklist

- [ ] Capture a clear idea and intended viewer value.
- [ ] Research carefully and keep source links.
- [ ] Create a short outline.
- [ ] Prepare and record/edit at a realistic scale.
- [ ] Review accuracy, audio, credits, and privacy.
- [ ] Publish with intentional visibility and accurate details.
- [ ] Record the link and one lesson learned.

## Keep it manageable

Plan one video at a time. It is okay to pause, change the outline, or decide not to publish. The purpose of a workflow is to make creative work easier—not to add pressure.
